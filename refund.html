    Our policy regarding course fees and refunds

    1. Overview

    This policy explains when and how refunds can be requested at EduLearn.

    2. Eligibility for Refund

    • Request within 7 days of purchase
    • Less than 20% of course content completed
    • Not a special non-refundable offer

    3. Non-Refundable Cases

    • Request after 7 days
    • More than 20% course accessed
    • Account sharing or Terms violation

    4. How to Request

    Email support@edulearn.com with:

    1. Full name and registered email
    2. Course name and purchase date
    3. Reason for refund

    We respond within 5–7 working days.

    5. Refund Process

    Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–14 working days.

    6. Contact

    Questions? support@edulearn.com or use the Contact page.
